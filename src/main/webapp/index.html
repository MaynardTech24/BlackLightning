Martial Arts • Discipline • Self-Defense • Character

Black Lightning is a martial arts organization dedicated to developing discipline, physical fitness, confidence, self-defense skills, and character through structured martial arts training.


We offer training in Lightning Scientific Arnis International, Traditional Karate, Kyokushin Karate, Full Contact Karate, and Combat Arnis.
