MEMBERSHIP FEE

₱3,500

The Black Lightning Martial Arts membership requires a one-time payment of ₱3,500 for individuals who wish to become official students of the organization.

Membership Benefits

  1. One-Time Payment
    The membership fee is ₱3,500, payable only once.
  2. Official Student Recognition
    Once the membership fee has been paid, you will be officially recognized as a student of Black Lightning Martial Arts and its affiliated martial arts organizations.

Affiliated Organizations

  • Lightning Scientific Arnis International
  • Lightning Scientific Martial Art Culture
  • IKOKS Sonoda
  • Combat Judo-Karate Association of the Philippines
  • PUMA Sikadsu

Authorized Uniform and Organization Shirt

Official members are allowed to wear the Karate Gi and the organization's official T-shirt, subject to the organization's rules and guidelines.

Trial Training Option — ₱250 Per Session

Black Lightning Martial Arts also offers trial training for individuals who want to experience martial arts training before becoming members.

  • Training Fee: ₱250 per training session.
  • No Immediate Membership Required: You may attend training without paying the ₱3,500 membership fee immediately.
  • Student Status: Trial participants are not yet officially recognized as students of the affiliated organizations.
  • Uniform Restrictions: Trial participants are not allowed to wear the Karate Gi until they become official members.

Choose Your Training Option

You may start with trial training for ₱250 per session to experience our training program. If you wish to become an official student and receive the benefits of membership, you may register by paying the one-time membership fee of ₱3,500.

TRAINING FEE RATES

₱250 / Session

The training fee for both members and trial participants is ₱250 per training session.

Training Fee Breakdown

  • Members — ₱250 per session: Official members pay ₱250 each time they attend training.
  • Trial Participants — ₱250 per session: Individuals who want to try martial arts training before registering for membership also pay ₱250 per session.
NOTE:

The ₱250 training fee is separate from the ₱3,500 one-time membership fee. Trial participants are not yet considered official students and are not allowed to wear the Karate Gi until they become official members.
